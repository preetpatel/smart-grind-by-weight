B12 Informed — the contextual UI, worked through

The whole flow in the B12 language, then the two questions that decide whether it works: what belongs under the hero, and what the screen says when it has nothing to say.

The rule this direction lives by. The context block is one hairline and at most two rows, always in the same place, always the same size. It never pushes the dose around and it never grows a third row. When there is nothing true to say, it disappears entirely and the screen is simply B1 — which is why the empty states below matter more than the full ones.

How a row is written. Left is a noun, right is a value. The row has to survive being read as “Label: value” and read on its own. Never split a sentence across the two columns — “Then · restarts itself” and “Safe to · try again” read as broken English, because the eye takes the left column as a category and the right as data. Anything that is a sentence becomes a full-width note under the heading; anything that is an instruction goes in the action button’s subtitle. Values are numbers, states or times — never verbs.

Everything shown is data the firmware already has, except bean bag and roast date (which need an entry point) — freshness comes from freshness_hrs, accuracy from get_avg_accuracy_g(), the backlog from CloudSync, warnings from DiagnosticsController, and the live weight from the load cell that is running anyway.
