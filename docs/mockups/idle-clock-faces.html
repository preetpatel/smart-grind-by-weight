How to use this. The sidebar retunes every panel at once, so you can judge a size, a weight or a colour across the whole set rather than one at a time. Click any panel to see it at 1.7×. Star the ones worth pursuing and hit Copy starred — paste the result back to me and I'll build them properly.

Font reality. lv_conf.h compiles Montserrat 14/16/24/28/32/36/48/56/60 today. Anything larger needs a generated font next to the existing custom 56/60 declarations; a digits-only subset costs a few KB rather than the ~40 KB of a full face. The size slider tells you which options survive being pulled back under 60 px.

Burn-in. Large fixed shapes — rings, rails, frames — are riskier than small centred type, because the ±8 px shift is small relative to the shape. Options in Minimal are designed as a second stage after USER_IDLE_CLOCK_DIM_DELAY_MS rather than as faces in their own right. Seconds and a blinking colon both mean a redraw every second — cheap for the CPU, worse for a panel that sits lit for hours.
