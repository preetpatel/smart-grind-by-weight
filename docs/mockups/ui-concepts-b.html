Direction B — twelve variants

Same palette, same manners, twelve different postures. Each shown across the primary flow: Ready, Grinding, Complete. 280 × 456 at 1:1.

What's constant. True black, ink #f7f7f5, amber #f0b429, Montserrat, and the rule that exactly one action per screen gets colour. What varies is posture: where the content sits, how big the number is, how the action presents itself, and whether structure comes from rules, surfaces or pure space.

What to look for. Whether the dose is legible from two metres; whether the action reads as the action without being read; and whether Grinding and Complete still feel like the same machine as Ready. A variant that only works on one of the three screens isn't a direction, it's a screen.
