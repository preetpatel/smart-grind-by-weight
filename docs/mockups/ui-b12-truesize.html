How big is this screen really?

Waveshare 1.64″ AMOLED · 280 × 456 px · 21.8 × 35.5 mm of glass · ~326 PPI. Calibrate below, then judge the type at the size it will actually be.

3.78 px/mm
Hold a bank card against this box and drag the slider until the width matches — 85.6 mm

Until you do that, everything is drawn at the browser's nominal 96 DPI, which is usually 10–30% off. The panel outline below is the real thing: 21.8 mm across.

The reference points. At 326 PPI, one millimetre is 12.8 device pixels. watchOS — which runs at the same 326 PPI — puts its minimum legible size at 12 pt, and the Apple Watch's own smallest UI text is about 24 device px. The firmware's existing ladder starts at 14 px and does its real work at 24 px, which is exactly that floor. Anything below 24 px on this panel is texture, not text.

What this means for the mocks. Every concept gallery so far rendered 280 px wide on a ~110 PPI monitor — about 65 mm across, three times life size. Heroes and buttons survive the correction; 9.5–13 px labels do not.
