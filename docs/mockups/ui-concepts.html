Firmware UI — concept directions

Every panel is the real 280 × 456 at 1:1, Montserrat only, true black. Nothing here is built yet.

How to read this. The first four blocks are competing design directions — the same six screens drawn four ways. Pick a direction, or pick pieces from several. The last three blocks are interaction models for the screens where the layout isn't the real question: how you choose a dose, what you watch while it grinds, and how the menu is shaped.

Everything here is Montserrat, because that is what the firmware has. Where a direction wants a monospace or a different face, that's a generated font — a few KB for a digits-only subset, ~40 KB for a full one. No icons are drawn from LVGL's built-in symbol font; the glyph-like marks here are drawn shapes, which is what a custom icon set would mean in practice.
